\section{Complete stratified contrasts}
\label{app:stratified}

Each contrast below is recomputed independently inside every combination of benchmark,
model size and reasoning budget. These tables are the full evidence behind the
stratum-consistency digest of Table~\ref{tab:stratum-digest} and the caterpillar plot of
Figure~\ref{fig:stratum-caterpillar}.

Individual rows should be read as localisation rather than as inference. The
matched-pair count is reported for every row, and in many strata it is small; a stratum
resting on one or two matched pairs identifies where an effect lives but does not
establish it there.

Three metrics are tabulated for each of the four stratified axes: accuracy, vote-fraction
calibration error and the cost proxy. The remaining six metrics carried in the source table
(per-agent calibration error, final-producer calibration error, both deltas, coordination
efficiency and error amplification) are available in
\code{analysis/tables/axis\_relationships/axis\_stratified\_contrasts.csv} and are not
reproduced here.
