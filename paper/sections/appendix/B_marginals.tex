\section{Complete marginal means and matched contrasts}
\label{app:marginals}

This appendix carries the complete benchmark-resolved marginal means and matched adjacent
contrasts for every outcome metric. Marginal means average completed seeds within a design
cell and then design cells within a level, with hierarchical qid-then-seed bootstrap
percentile intervals. Matched contrasts are formed only from cell pairs agreeing on every
other factor and on seed. In the contrast tables, $\ast$ marks an interval excluding zero.
