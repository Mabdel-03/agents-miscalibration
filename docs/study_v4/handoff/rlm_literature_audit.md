# RLM literature and artifact audit for revision 4

Checked September 5, 2026. Primary sources were inspected directly; versions and limits below matter. This bounded audit identifies substantial existing coverage and a defensible experimental direction, not a certificate of global novelty. Section7 of the companion v4 specification is the proposed study specification, not a description of completed experiments.

## Two SRLM implementation issues worth preserving in the audit

The published score discussion says lower is better but the displayed decision maximizes the score. Also, after restricting to trajectories with one normalized plurality answer, reranking those trajectories cannot change that answer. These are reasons to pin/test actual code or label an adaptation; they do not negate the substantive prior question. The study should separately report generation changes, complete-trajectory supply and final answer selection. A confidence/length comparator must not be credited with an answer improvement that its decision rule cannot produce. [Method §2.2.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.15653v1)

## Residual claim and submission audit

The defensible candidate question is whether different integration/runtime policies preserve information that remains useful for subsequent computation, and whether a content-grounded neural signal improves the choice of that subsequent computation beyond observable uncertainty, text, verified evidence and ordinary repeated program search under a complete global allowance. It is not the broad claim that recursion, external memory, fresh roots or confidence helps.

Before novelty wording freezes, inspect any newer versions of these papers, trace relevant citations, and audit accessible code for the closest baseline semantics. Record `verified_overlap`, `residual_with_limit`, or `unknown`; do not turn inaccessible code into a negative literature finding. A direct duplication discovered later should narrow the claim to replication, boundary condition or a concrete extension without invalidating useful pilot work. The proposed v4 controls make the question testable; only results and a final literature update can establish the strength of the contribution.
